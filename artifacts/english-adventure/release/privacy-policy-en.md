This app is made for children. This policy is written so a parent can read it
in three minutes and know exactly where they stand.

## The short version

English Adventure does not collect any personal information about your child or
about you. Nothing your child does in the app leaves the phone. There is no
advertising, no account, and no tracking of any kind.

## What we collect

Nothing.

The app does not ask for and does not collect names of any legal significance,
email addresses, phone numbers, photographs, location, contacts, device
identifiers, or advertising IDs.

## What stays on your phone

To teach your child, the app records on the device only:

- the nickname and age range you choose when setting up a profile
- which sounds, words and letters your child has practised, and how it went
- practice time and the stars earned
- tracing attempts, so the parent dashboard can show handwriting progress

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your own device. We
cannot see it. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.
The parent area has an offline "Delete all data" action. After a confirmation,
it permanently deletes every child profile, session, practice attempt, skill
state, reward, handwriting trace, and queued record stored by the app, then
returns to a fresh starting state. Deleting the app also deletes all of it
permanently.

## Advertising

There is none. No banner ads, no video ads, no sponsored content, no
cross-promotion of other apps, and no third-party advertising software of any
kind is included in this app.

## Purchases

[IF THE MVP HAS NO PURCHASES:] This version contains no purchases.
[IF/WHEN PAID CONTENT IS ADDED:] Any purchase is made from the parent area,
behind a parent gate that a young child cannot pass. Payment is handled entirely
by Google Play or the App Store; we never see or store card details.

## Links out of the app

A child using the app cannot reach any external website, store page, or social
media. The few links that exist — this policy and our contact address — sit
behind the parent gate.

## Analytics

This version contains no analytics software.

If anonymous learning analytics are added in a future version, they will be
switched off by default and will only ever run after a parent switches them on
in the parent area. They would record anonymous events such as "an activity was
completed", never anything identifying a child.

## Cloud sync

This version has no cloud sync. Everything is on your device.

If optional sync is added later, a parent will have to create an account and
switch it on deliberately. Even then, only anonymous progress records would sync
— a child's name would not.

## Children's privacy

This app is directed to children and is designed to comply with Google Play's
Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age. We do not use
any child's data for advertising, profiling, or behavioural targeting. We do not
sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

## Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show you or delete
on our side. To remove everything the app stores on your device without an
internet connection, use the parent-gated "Delete all data" action. You can also
uninstall the app.

## Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example if optional sync is added — we will update
this page and the "last updated" date above, and the app will ask a parent
before anything new begins.
