# Play Console → App content — every answer, ready to enter

Wrong answers here get apps removed, and for a children's app the review is
stricter. These are the answers for the MVP as built: no network, no accounts,
no analytics, no ads.

## Data safety form

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | N/A (no data leaves the device) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **Yes** — the parent-gated, offline parent area → Delete all data action removes every locally stored child record, and uninstalling removes everything |

Resulting store label: **No data collected**. That is a real advantage in this
category — say so in your listing.

> The moment you add cloud sync or analytics, this form must change **before**
> that build is rolled out. Sync = "Collects: app activity, linked to the user".
> Analytics = "Collects: app interactions, not linked to the user".

## Target audience and content

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Target age groups | **6–8** and **9–12** (do not tick 13+; ticking any adult bracket takes you out of Families) |
| Is your app designed for children? | **Yes — children only** |
| Do you want to apply for the Teacher Approved programme? | **Yes** |
| Does your app show ads? | **No** |
| Ads: does your app contain ads in any form? | **No** |

Selecting a children-only audience makes the **Families Policy** binding:
no ads SDK without certification, no cross-promotion, no external links reachable
by a child, a compliant privacy policy, and a neutral age screen if you ever add
one. The app as built already satisfies all of these.
