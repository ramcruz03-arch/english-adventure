## Full description (4000 char max)

English Adventure teaches a child to read English one sound at a time, in a
quiet garden with a friendly guide called Anil.

It is built for the child who finds reading hard. There are no timers, no
lives, no scores and no leaderboards anywhere in this app. Nothing counts down.
Nothing can be lost. A child who gets every answer wrong still finishes her
session with a star and a kind word, because the reward is for showing up and
trying — never for being right.

WHAT YOUR CHILD LEARNS

• Letter shapes, uppercase and lowercase
• The sound each letter makes — taught as sounds, not letter names
• Hearing the first and last sound in a word
• Blending sounds together into real words: cat, sun, map, pen
• Building and spelling simple words
• Tracing and handwriting
• Reading short sentences and simple illustrated stories

We follow systematic synthetic phonics, the approach used in schools across the
UK and India. Sounds are taught in a careful order, and your child is never
shown a word containing a sound nobody has taught her yet.

HOW IT WORKS

Each day is one short adventure of ten to fifteen minutes, made of three to
five small activities rather than one long lesson. New learning, revision,
reading and writing are mixed together. Sounds your child finds difficult come
back more often, automatically.

When something is hard, the app helps rather than marks. One miss brings a
hint. Two brings a demonstration. Three ends the activity warmly and makes the
next one easier. Your child is never told she is wrong, slow, or behind.

FOR PARENTS

A private dashboard, behind a parent gate, shows practice time, which sounds
are mastered, which words need more work, and how handwriting is coming along.
It shows growth, never a rank or a comparison with other children. It also
suggests simple things to do together away from the screen.

BUILT FOR CHILDREN WHO NEED PATIENCE

Large buttons, plenty of space, a warm low-glare background, and adjustable
font, text size and letter spacing — helpful for children with dyslexia-like
difficulties. Every letter, word, instruction and sentence can be heard aloud,
as many times as your child likes. British English pronunciation by default,
with Indian English available.

PRIVACY

• No advertising of any kind
• No links your child can tap out to
• No account, no email, no phone number required
• Nothing is collected about your child and nothing leaves the phone
• Works fully offline after download

Parent guidance is available in Tamil while your child's learning stays in
English.

Made for children aged 5 to 9 who are beginning to read, and for the parents
sitting beside them.
